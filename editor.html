WORLD / AUDIO

Primary plays at race start, then alternate plays when it ends (and they keep rotating). Both are selectable in Pause. A global soundtrack override takes precedence.

Images come from public/images. For the correct wrap, use a 2:1 equirectangular panorama.

ROAD / RAILS

Optional image from public/images, repeated over the road. Leave blank to use the material's built-in look.

CPU DIFFICULTY CALIBRATION

Per-track multiplier for CPU top speed, acceleration and braking. 1.00 keeps the existing balance; Rookie/Pilot/Elite still apply.

SELECTED POINT —

Empty = global width. Width blends smoothly between points.

With magnetic road enabled, 0 → 90 → 180 → 270 → 360 makes a full roll. Keep 360 on later points to stay upright.

ALTERNATIVE ROUTES

Choose an approach side to take a branch. Start/end are shared lap %. Amber road is the alternative route. Its endpoints follow the main road.

GAPS 0

Orange = takeoff, green = landing. Positions are % of one lap. Leave room to accelerate and land.

ROAD MARKINGS & PADS

Green chevrons accelerate; orange bars reduce speed once by 28%. Positions are shared lap %. Editing pads replaces automatic placement. Suggestions add boosts only on clear acceleration corridors. Purple weapon pads are Combat only. With no authored weapon pads, automatic pickups are previewed; adding one replaces that fallback.
