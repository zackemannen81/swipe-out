# THE TOUR BUS

## FIRST VISIT – CREW ANNOUNCEMENTS

### 00 – WELCOME ABOARD

"Welcome aboard THE TOUR BUS.

The official interstellar transport vessel of the Anti-Gravity World Championship.

For the duration of the tournament, this vessel will serve as your home, your headquarters, and your gateway to the competing worlds.

We invite you to explore our facilities, meet your fellow pilots, and prepare for the challenges ahead.

Your comfort is our priority.

Your survival... is your responsibility.

Enjoy your stay."

---

### 01 – GRAND HANGAR

*Main hangar / Flight operations*

"Welcome to the Grand Hangar.

This is the operational heart of The Tour Bus, where championship racing vessels are stored, serviced, and prepared for deployment.

Your assigned craft is available for inspection and customization in the adjoining Service Bay.

Please refrain from activating your propulsion systems while inside the hangar.

The structural repair department has requested this announcement.

Repeatedly."

---

### 02 – PILOTS' LOUNGE

*Social hub / Rival pilots*

"This is the Pilots' Lounge.

Here, representatives of the championship's four competing civilizations may relax, exchange information, and engage in diplomatic conversation.

We encourage you to become acquainted with your fellow pilots.

Each of them is absolutely convinced that their civilization will emerge victorious.

Statistically speaking...

Most of them are mistaken.

Please enjoy the refreshments."

---

### 03 – WORKSHOP / SERVICE BAY

*Craft customization / Upgrades*

"Welcome to the Service Bay.

Our engineering facilities provide everything required to maintain, customize, and enhance your racing vessel.

Select your preferred craft, modify its appearance, and install performance modules to suit your driving style.

Please remember that increased performance may come at the expense of stability, efficiency, or control.

The engineering department accepts no responsibility for modifications performed without adequate consideration.

We do, however, appreciate the resulting data."

---

### 04 – SPARE PARTS MARKET

*Equipment / Special components*

"Welcome to the Spare Parts Market.

Here, pilots may acquire specialized components, equipment, and cosmetic enhancements from across the participating civilizations.

Certain items are available for purchase.

Others must be earned through exceptional performance, local challenges, or personal agreements.

Please inspect all components carefully before installation.

Refunds are subject to availability.

Availability is not guaranteed."

---

### 05 – TOURNAMENT NEXUS

*Briefing / Championship operations*

"Welcome to the Tournament Nexus.

This facility coordinates all official championship events, planetary destinations, and race operations.

Here you may review upcoming competitions, examine participating civilizations, and select your next assignment.

Authorized surface visits are also available.

Please ensure that your vessel is properly configured before entering an event.

Remember...

An entire world is depending on your performance.

No pressure."

---

### 06 – OBSERVATION DECK

*Planetary observation / Exploration*

"Welcome to the Observation Deck.

From here, you may observe our current planetary destination and the extraordinary civilization that calls it home.

The observation systems provide information about local environments, cultural characteristics, and available surface activities.

We encourage all passengers to appreciate the remarkable beauty of the universe.

While the opportunity remains.

Enjoy the view."

---

### 07 – DIPLOMATIC CORRIDOR

*Faction history / Interstellar diplomacy*

"Welcome to the Diplomatic Corridor.

This gallery honors the four great civilizations participating in the championship.

Each banner represents an extraordinary history of innovation, achievement, and unwavering belief in its own superiority.

We encourage you to explore their philosophies and traditions.

Please remember that diplomatic neutrality is strictly enforced aboard this vessel.

Personal opinions are permitted.

Armed disagreements are not."

---

### 08 – CREW QUARTERS

*Crew accommodation / Shipboard life*

"Welcome to the Crew Quarters.

These facilities provide accommodation and essential services for the personnel responsible for maintaining our vessel.

Our dedicated crew works continuously to ensure the championship proceeds according to schedule.

Please respect designated rest periods and restricted personal areas.

The crew has been informed that their contribution is invaluable.

Additional compensation has not been discussed.

Thank you for your cooperation."

---

### 09 – MEDBAY

*Medical services / Recovery*

"Welcome to the Medical Bay.

Our advanced medical facilities are equipped to assist with injuries, physiological stress, and other unfortunate consequences of high-velocity competition.

Recovery services are available to authorized personnel throughout the tournament.

We recommend reporting any unusual symptoms immediately.

This includes dizziness, disorientation, and temporary loss of consciousness.

Excessive confidence, however...

Remains untreatable."

---

### 10 – ARCHIVE / LORE CHAMBER

*History / Records / Replays*

"Welcome to the Championship Archive.

Within these halls, the histories of participating civilizations, remarkable achievements, and significant tournament events are preserved for future generations.

Here you may review discovered records, revisit presentations, and explore information gathered throughout your journey.

Access to certain documents requires additional clearance.

Please note that the archive contains only officially authorized information.

Historical accuracy is a matter of administrative policy."

*Power systems / Ship operations*

"Welcome to Engineering.

This section houses the primary systems responsible for maintaining the vessel's propulsion, environmental stability, and operational power distribution.

Our engineering personnel continuously monitor thousands of interconnected processes to ensure uninterrupted functionality.

For your safety, access to critical systems is restricted.

Please avoid touching unfamiliar equipment.

Particularly equipment that appears to be touching you.

Thank you."

*Equipment / Logistics*

"Welcome to Cargo and Storage.

This facility manages the enormous quantity of equipment, materials, and replacement components required to support an interstellar championship.

All cargo is catalogued, inspected, and assigned according to operational priority.

Authorized personnel may retrieve approved equipment from designated storage areas.

Please do not open containers marked as restricted.

Their contents are not relevant to your participation.

Have a pleasant day."

*Planetary transfer / Surface exploration*

"Welcome to the Shuttle Bay.

The Tour Bus operates according to its own schedule.
