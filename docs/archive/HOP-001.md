## Findings (2026-11-03)
Inspected ship-physics.js, race-interactions.js, game.js, track-path.js and both
camera implementations. Magnetic surface driving resets vertical offset and
velocity to zero; gap flight is separate. Nonmagnetic crest logic can initiate
upward motion. Swipeout magnetic follow camera applies a minimum local clearance
of 1.6 after world-space lag; this is a candidate for perceived height corrections,
not a confirmed diagnosis. Rail impacts and racer collisions can also cause jolts.
No reproduction or code changes yet. Asked user for hop direction, track and
whether it occurs near edges or on the open road. Awaiting that distinction.
